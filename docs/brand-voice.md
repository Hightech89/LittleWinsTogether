# Brand Voice

## Emotional Goal
Little Wins Together should feel calm, safe, reassuring, and human.

This is not a corporate product, productivity app, or high-energy startup brand.

It is a supportive community for parents raising children with autism.

## Tone Guidelines

Use language that feels:
- gentle
- clear
- warm
- steady
- respectful

Avoid language that feels:
- salesy
- overly excited
- competitive
- exaggerated
- corporate

## Preferred Language

Prefer phrases like:
- supportive space
- little wins
- community
- understanding
- encouragement
- support
- connection

## Avoid These Kinds of Phrases

Avoid phrases like:
- success stories
- unlock
- level up
- crush it
- thrive at all costs
- supercharge
- growth hacks

## Design Tone

The interface should feel:
- calm
- breathable
- simple
- spacious
- soft

Use:
- generous whitespace
- rounded corners
- soft blue accents
- gentle contrast
- clear typography

Avoid:
- loud colors
- sharp, aggressive styles
- cluttered layouts
- dark, heavy visual tone

## Homepage Direction

Hero headline:
Celebrate Every Little Win Together

The words "Little Win" should be highlighted in blue.

Subheading:
A supportive space for parents raising children with autism.

The page should lead with quiet reassurance, not urgency.
